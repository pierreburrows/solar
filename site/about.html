Who we are

Installations we'd be proud to have at home

Our engineers have extensive experience in domestic and commercial electrical work and hold the latest qualifications and accreditations for EV installations.

Our customers in Brighton, Sussex and across the South East know we know our stuff. We're passionate about EV technology and committed to the highest quality installations, ones we'd be proud to have in our own homes and workplaces.

EVelec Ltd is fully qualified and insured to install Solar PV and battery storage, from small domestic systems to larger commercial premises. We help customers take the worry out of high energy bills and harness the power and convenience of renewable energy.
