OZEV EV Charge Point Grant

Residents of rental properties, and owners of flats or apartments with designated parking, are eligible for up to £350 off the cost of an EV charge point (subject to meeting all eligibility criteria).

  • Flat & apartment owners with designated parking
  • Tenants in rental properties
  • Deducted straight from your installation cost
At work
£14,000

Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS)

£350 per socket, up to 40 sockets, for commercial premises serving staff, customers and visitors, or both.

  • £350 per socket
  • Up to 40 sockets
  • For staff, customers & visitors
How it works

We handle the paperwork

  • Tell us about your property and parking
  • We confirm eligibility and recommend a charger
  • We apply for the grant on your behalf
  • Our engineers install and certify your charge point
Please note: all grants are subject to eligibility criteria set by the Office for Zero Emission Vehicles (OZEV), and schemes and amounts can change. Get in touch and we'll confirm exactly what you're entitled to.
